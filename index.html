Polaris Workspace

A private, personal-use integration for its owner's own Google account.

What this app does

Polaris Workspace is a personal automation tool. It is used by a single person (its owner) to read and manage their own Google account data: Gmail messages and drafts, Google Calendar events, and files in Google Drive (including Sheets and Docs), plus basic Google Contacts lookups.

Typical uses are practical and personal: filing and drafting email, keeping a family calendar in sync, generating a printable weekly schedule, and organizing documents in Drive. There is no public sign-up, no multi-user access, and no service offered to anyone other than the owner.

How it works

The app runs on hardware controlled by its owner and connects to Google APIs using OAuth 2.0. It acts only on the owner's own account and only when the owner invokes it. No data is sent to third parties, and no data is sold or shared.
