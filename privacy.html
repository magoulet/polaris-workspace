Privacy Policy

Polaris Workspace · Last updated: September 27, 2026

Polaris Workspace ("the app") is a private, personal automation tool operated by and for a single individual (its owner). This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses, how that data is used, and how it is protected.

1. What data is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, the app may access the owner's own Google account data, limited to:

  • Gmail - read messages, create and manage drafts, and send email on the owner's behalf
  • Google Calendar - read and create calendar events
  • Google Drive - read and manage files
  • Google Sheets and Docs - read and edit the owner's spreadsheets and documents
  • Google Contacts - read-only lookups of the owner's contacts

2. How the data is used

Data is used solely to perform tasks the owner requests of the app on their own account, such as organizing email, drafting messages, maintaining a personal calendar, and generating documents. The app does not use this data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose beyond the owner's direct instructions.

3. Sharing and disclosure

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party. The app transfers Google user data only between Google's APIs and the owner's own devices. No third-party analytics, ad, or tracking services are used.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and any cached content are stored locally on hardware the owner controls. Data is retained only as long as needed for the owner's personal use and can be deleted at any time by the owner.

5. Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Security

Access is granted through Google OAuth 2.0. Credentials are stored with restricted file permissions on the owner's own devices and are never shared publicly. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

7. Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app's data handling can be sent to magoulet@gmail.com.
